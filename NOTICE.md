# Notices

RF-5 is an independent RackForge plugin implemented in Rust and distributed
under GPL-3.0-only.

The project studies a historically significant five-voice analog synthesizer
architecture for interoperability and technical research. RF-5 is not
affiliated with or endorsed by any hardware manufacturer. No third-party
firmware, ROM, factory sound bank, product artwork or trademark is included in
the source repository or plugin package.
